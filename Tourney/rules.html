📋 TOURNAMENT RULES 📋

Official competition format and guidelines

⚔️ Tournament Format

Arcane Tournaments uses the EWC Match Point Format - the same competitive format used in elite Call of Duty Warzone competitions worldwide.

Match Point Threshold: 135 Points
Victory Condition: First team to reach 135 points AND win a match becomes champion

Teams compete in Warzone Resurgence Trios matches, accumulating points through eliminations and placement bonuses. The tournament continues until a team at Match Point secures a victory.

📝 Registration & Entry

  • Entry Fee: $15 per team (3 players)
  • Team Composition: Teams must consist of exactly 3 players
  • Registration: Use Discord bot command /register_team
  • Streaming: Teams can stream on Twitch, YouTube, or Kick
  • Payment: CashApp, Venmo, or PayPal accepted
  • Deadline: Registration closes 1 hour before tournament start

⚖️ Competition Rules

  • All players must use their registered Activision IDs
  • Teams submit match results via /submit_match command
  • Screenshot or video proof may be requested for high-kill matches
  • Cheating, hacking, or exploiting will result in immediate disqualification
  • Tournament admins have final say on all disputes
  • Teams must be ready to queue within 5 minutes of match call

📞 Support & Contact

For questions, disputes, or technical issues, contact tournament admins via Discord. Use the /ask command for quick answers about tournament rules and format.

Good luck, soldiers. May the best squad win.
